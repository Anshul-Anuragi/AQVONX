# BidSniper AI Chrome Extension Guide

BidSniper AI is engineered for freelancers, agencies, and independent consultants to analyze freelance job postings, extract hidden client names from past reviews, compute deal health scores, and write 150-word anti-AI proposals.

---

## 1. Core Capabilities

- **Client Detective**: Scans historical freelancer reviews using regex heuristics to uncover the client's actual first name before bidding.
- **Deal Health Scoring**: Computes a 0-100 Deal Health score based on hire rate, historical hourly spend, and payment verification.
- **Toxic Client Warnings**: Detects scope creep, micromanagement, and dispute patterns from past freelancer feedback.
- **Portfolio RAG Matching**: Automatically scores and matches your top-converting portfolio projects against the job requirements.
- **Anti-AI Proposal Generator**: Crafts razor-sharp, 130-175 word human proposals with custom 4-layer System Architecture Blueprints.
