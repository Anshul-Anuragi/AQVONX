# EditorialLens Chrome Extension Guide

EditorialLens is designed for civil services aspirants, researchers, and policy analysts to dissect long-form editorials, extract arguments, and map claims to syllabus topics.

---

## 1. Core Capabilities

- **Syllabus Point Mapping**: Instantly identifies relevant GS Paper (GS-1 through GS-4) modules mentioned in the article.
- **Multidimensional Arguments**: Breaks arguments into Pro, Con, Way Forward, and constitutional provisions.
- **Authoritative Citations**: Extracts references to Supreme Court cases, Law Commission reports, and committee recommendations.
- **One-Click Anki Sync**: Automatically formats quotes into spaced-repetition flashcards.

---

## 2. Supported Publications

- *The Hindu*
- *The Indian Express*
- *LiveMint*
- *PIB (Press Information Bureau)* releases

---

## 3. Activation & Token

EditorialLens communicates directly with the AQVONX FastAPI proxy using your hardware-locked JWT bearer token. Get your token from the [Dashboard Console](/dashboard).
